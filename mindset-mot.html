(02) Inside the hourHover the dial
60Minutes, roughly like this

What happens in your MOT.

Every Mindset MOT is different, because every person is. But here's roughly how the hour flows.

~10 min

Get to know each other

No forms, no scripts. Just a proper conversation.

~20 min

What's going on, and what you want

I'll ask some questions to find out what's happening for you and what you want to achieve.

~20 min

Where you're stuck, plus one tool

I'll show you where you're stuck, and you'll leave with an insight, tip or tool to move forward.

~10 min

Your questions, your call

Ask me anything. If you're interested, I'll share what working together could look like. Without pressure.

(03) Is it for you?Honest answers

Built for high achievers and people pleasers.

I truly believe in the transformational power of coaching, so it's my pleasure to give you a taster, and for you to leave with a sense of renewed possibility.

It's for you if…

  • You're a high achiever who's never quite satisfied with your own efforts
  • You say yes when you mean no, and put yourself last
  • You overthink, procrastinate or hold yourself to impossible standards
  • You've tried books, journalling, even therapy, and nothing's really shifted

You'll leave with…

  • A clearer view of what's really keeping you stuck
  • One insight, tip or tool you can use straight away
  • A fresh sense of renewed possibility
  • Clarity on whether working together is right for you, with zero pressure
