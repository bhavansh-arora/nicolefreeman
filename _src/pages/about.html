(01) Because you're worth itMy story

I always loved that L'Oréal ad, “because you're worth it.” But for many years, I felt I wasn't worth it. I put myself down. I prioritised everyone else's needs over my own. I was my own harshest critic, and nothing I did was ever good enough.

I compared myself endlessly to others, personally and professionally. Then coaching and hypnotherapy helped me learn that all those “stories” in my head were just that: stories, which could be rewritten.

Doing that helped me overcome my overthinking. Procrastination, people pleasing and perfectionism no longer hold me back. I found my voice, changed career, and grew in confidence. Above all, I'm happier, calmer and more present.

That's true freedom. And if I can do it, so can you. Let's get you out there, showing the world how you shine, free from the shackles of always worrying whether you're good enough.

Because you absolutely are.

You don't have to be perfect to be amazing.

Nicole Freeman
