(06) Good questionsFAQ

Before you book.

Can't see your question? Ask me anything in your free Mindset MOT, or send a message.

Send a message
What happens in the free Mindset MOT?

We have an hour to get to know each other. I'll ask some questions to find out what's going on for you and what you want to achieve, show you where you're stuck, and you'll leave with an insight, tip or tool to move you forward. If you're interested, I'll share what working together could look like. Without pressure.

Is hypnotherapy like stage hypnosis?

Not at all. Clinical hypnotherapy is a calm, focused and deeply relaxed state. You're aware throughout and you stay in control. It's simply a gentler way to reach the beliefs that sit beneath conscious thought.

Do I need to be in London?

No. Sessions work brilliantly on Zoom, so you can work with me from anywhere. If you are in London, we can also meet in person or do a walk and talk.

How long does coaching take?

Packages generally run for 6 to 12 months, with sessions weekly or fortnightly. That rhythm gives you time to put new thinking into action between sessions, which is where lasting change happens.

What is a DISC profile?

DISC is a personality profile that shows how you naturally communicate, make decisions and handle pressure. It's included in my packages and gives us a useful shared language from the start.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

Coaching is forward-focused: changing how you think, feel and react so you can act differently and reach your goals. If something comes up that needs a different kind of support, I'll always tell you honestly.
